# Product Requirements
Lush AI Marketing Platform

## Primary Objective
Build a premium internal AI-powered marketing operating system for Lush Trade Corp Tanzania Limited.

## Core Modules

1. **Dashboard**
   - Central overview: leads, pipeline, content, campaigns, tasks, market opportunities.
   - Strictly NO fake data. Use empty states.

2. **AI Business Brain**
   - Central knowledge system: company info, products, markets, tone, claims, documents.

3. **Content Studio**
   - Content generation tailored by product, audience, platform, and objective.

4. **Reel / Creative Studio**
   - Hooks, scripts, storyboards, voiceovers.

5. **Market Research**
   - Research countries, commodities, demand, competitors.
   - Must include source, date, confidence, and evidence.

6. **Lead Finder & CRM**
   - Discover B2B companies (importers, distributors, processors).
   - CRM pipeline: New -> Researching -> Qualified -> Contacted -> Engaged -> Negotiation -> Won/Lost/Nurture.
   - Lead scoring with explicit reasoning.

7. **Campaigns & Outreach**
   - Manage cross-channel marketing campaigns.
   - Draft personalized outreach (Email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp).
   - **Mandatory Human Approval** for all outward communication.

8. **Website AI Assistant**
   - Answering website visitors using ONLY approved knowledge.

9. **Analytics**
   - Tracking performance across leads, campaigns, and content without hallucinated metrics.
