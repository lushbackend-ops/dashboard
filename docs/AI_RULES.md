# AI Rules & Factuality
Lush AI Marketing Platform

## ZERO HALLUCINATION POLICY
This is the most critical constraint of the platform. The AI must NEVER invent business facts.

### The AI MUST NEVER invent:
- Customers or Suppliers
- Prices, Product availability, or Shipment quantities
- Certifications, Contracts, or Testimonials
- Revenue, Profits, or Market share
- Delivery guarantees
- Lead contact information (Names, Phone numbers, Email addresses)
- Statistics, Trade volumes, or Regulatory approvals

### If information is unavailable:
The AI MUST state that it is unavailable.

### Allowed AI Operations (Marketing Content):
The AI may:
- Rewrite, summarize, structure, and improve text.
- Suggest and brainstorm ideas and campaigns.
- Generate hooks, captions, and scripts.
- Personalize content using **VERIFIED DATA**.

### Claim Verification
Claims like "CBT Registered", "SGS Inspected", or "TCCIA Verified" must be explicitly tracked with a status (`VERIFIED`, `NEEDS_REVIEW`, `UNVERIFIED`, `EXPIRED`). The AI must only use `VERIFIED` claims in definite marketing statements.

### Human-in-the-Loop
Human approval is absolutely required before:
- Sending external messages
- Publishing social content
- Making commercial claims
- Committing prices or shipment dates
