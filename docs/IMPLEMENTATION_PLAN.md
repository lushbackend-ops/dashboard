# Implementation Plan
Lush AI Marketing Platform

## Phase 1: Foundation
- Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind setup
- Project structure & routing
- Supabase integration & authentication
- Dashboard shell and navigation

## Phase 2: Lush AI Business Brain
- Company profile & product catalog
- Brand voice & approved claims system
- AI context retrieval (RAG)
- AI provider abstraction implementation

## Phase 3: Content Studio
- Content generator (captions, hooks, ideas)
- Platform-specific variations
- Approval workflow & storage

## Phase 4: Creative / Reel Studio
- Reel ideas, scripts, storyboards
- Canva integration verification and potential implementation

## Phase 5: Market Intelligence
- Market & competitor research modules
- Source tracking & evidence logging

## Phase 6: Lead Finder
- Company discovery and lead record creation
- Enrichment and verification tracking

## Phase 7: Lead Scoring + CRM
- Configurable scoring system with explanations
- CRM pipeline, notes, tasks, activities

## Phase 8: Campaigns
- Campaign builder (audience, products, content)

## Phase 9: Outreach
- Personalized drafts (Email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp)
- Human approval workflow for outbound messages

## Phase 10: Website AI
- AI assistant using strictly approved knowledge
- Enquiry capture and lead routing

## Phase 11: Analytics
- Marketing, lead, campaign, and pipeline analytics

## Phase 12: Automation
- n8n integration or background jobs for scheduled research/content
